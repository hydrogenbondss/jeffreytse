Creative Technologist.

Jeffrey N. Tse is an artist, creative technologist, and founder based in Hong Kong. His practice spans interactive narrative, generative systems, material culture research, and product design — examining how human presence is shaped by the technologies and structures we inhabit.

Trained in Art History at UBC and Marketing at PolyU Hong Kong. Curated five exhibitions across Vancouver and Hong Kong (2017–2019). Currently building RE/SENSE, running PawsAid — a pet first-aid brand he designed from product to platform — and maintaining ROLL CALL.
