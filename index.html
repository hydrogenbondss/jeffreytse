ABOUT
Practice

Interdisciplinary artist, systems designer, and researcher whose work investigates how human presence is reshaped through systems of observation, classification, memory, and mediation. Working across archival interfaces, curatorial practice, AI-assisted interaction systems, writing, and interactive media.

His practice began through curatorial and research-based investigations into intimacy, belonging, and spectatorship — across five exhibitions in Vancouver and Hong Kong. Published research has examined the ethics of documentary violence and how dating platforms reshape identity through systems of visibility and behavioral feedback.

Current works — including ROLL CALL, RE/SENSE, and PawsAid — involve archives, AI-assisted interaction, and longitudinal memory structures. Systems are approached not as neutral tools, but as environments that shape how people understand themselves and others.
