Jeffrey N. Tse is a Hong Kong-based Systems Architect, Art Director, and Editorial Strategist. He built PawsAid — three tiers of pet first-aid kits — from brand to platform to shipping product. He runs ROLL CALL, a material-culture archive that catalogues toilet paper like museum specimens, ply count and all. He's written the editorial voice behind a dozen-plus I.T Apparels campaigns, from A BATHING APE® to Carhartt WIP.

That throughline isn't incidental. ROLL CALL turns a bathroom object into a materials essay; years earlier, his undergraduate paper on Lawrence Beitler's 1930 lynching photograph asked the same question of a very different kind of evidence — what gets looked at, and what looking does.
