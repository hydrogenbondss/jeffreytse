About

Artist building at the edge of human systems.

Jeffrey N. Tse is an interdisciplinary artist, curator, and systems designer based in Hong Kong. His practice spans interactive narrative, generative systems, material culture research, and editorial work.

Trained in Art History at UBC and Marketing at PolyU Hong Kong. Has curated five exhibitions across Vancouver and Hong Kong since 2017. Currently building RE/SENSE, running PawsAid, and maintaining ROLL CALL — a 43-specimen archive of toilet paper across 21 countries.
