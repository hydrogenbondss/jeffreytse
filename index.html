About

Jeffrey N. Tse is an interdisciplinary artist, curator, and systems designer based in Hong Kong. His practice spans interactive narrative, generative systems, material culture research, and editorial work — examining how human presence is shaped by the technologies and structures we inhabit.

He holds an MSc in Marketing Management from Hong Kong Polytechnic University and a BA in Art History from the University of British Columbia. He has curated five exhibitions across Vancouver and Hong Kong, and his research has been cited internationally.
