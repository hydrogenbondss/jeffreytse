Hello —

Jeffrey N. Tse

Systems Architect · Art Director · Editorial Strategist

He builds the structure underneath a thing and the way it reads on the surface — identity systems, editorial direction, and narrative work — and treats the seam between the two as the whole job. Trained in Art History at Hong Kong Polytechnic University, he came to design through looking hard at objects and asking who made them, what they cost, and where they end up.

These days that runs from ECHO, an interactive narrative, to ROLL CALL, a material-culture archive, to SPECTOR and a steady habit of cataloguing the overlooked. Based in Hong Kong.
