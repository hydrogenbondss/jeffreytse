Finite Appearances

Co-Curator / Wil Aballe Art Projects / Vancouver / 2018

The body has always been mobilised as a site where presence is both inscribed and erased. Finite Appearances brought together works by Kelly Holmes made from imprinted bodily traces — plaster casts of hands, soap moulds, video of a snow angel, and a floor of plaster navels. The show asked whether intimacy can be represented in any medium at all, and whether the permanence of art contradicts the intimate act it preserves.

— can a cast hold a feeling? or just the shape it left ✦
