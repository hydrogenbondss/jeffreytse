The Offender Behind the Lens

The Ethical Legacy of Beitler’s Lynching Photograph
Jeffrey N. Tse — essay

A study of the ethical legacy of the photographic record of racial violence: the position of the lens, the responsibilities of looking, and what it means to circulate an image whose original act was the crime it documents.

Plate withheld

The lynching photograph at the centre of this essay is not reproduced. The argument is the ethics of circulating such an image — to show it would repeat the act the writing interrogates.

Cited  Milwaukee Independent, 2020 — on the strain of seeing images of anti-Black violence
