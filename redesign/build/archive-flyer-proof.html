The Offender Behind the Lens

The Ethical Legacy of Beitler's Lynching Photograph · Author: J. N. Tse

A study of the ethical legacy of the photographic record of racial violence — the position of the lens, the responsibilities of looking, and what it means to circulate an image whose original act was the crime it documents.

Cited in the Milwaukee Independent (2020), in its coverage of the emotional strain of seeing images and video of anti-Black violence.
