SPECTOR

An auto-paced teleprompter built for the smart-glasses era — rehearse on your phone, then perform eyes-up on Ray-Ban Meta, XREAL or Viture. Punctuation-aware adaptive pacing, a Comfort mode that spatially anchors the script with Kalman-filtered head-tracking, and post-run analytics on pacing, hesitation and WPM — all in a zero-dependency PWA.
