Finite Appearances

Kelly Holmes  ·  co-curated by Jeffrey N. Tse  ·  Wil Aballe Art Projects, Vancouver

Role
Co-Curator
Artist
Kelly Holmes
Year
2018
Venue
Wil Aballe Art Projects (WAAP)
City
Vancouver, CA
Medium
Plaster, soap, single-channel video

The body has always been mobilised as a site where presence is both inscribed and erased.

Finite Appearances brought together works by Kelly Holmes made from imprinted bodily traces — plaster casts of hands, soap moulds, a single-channel video of a snow angel, and a floor of plaster navels. Each object holds the negative space of a body that is no longer there.

The exhibition asked whether intimacy can be represented in any medium at all — and whether the permanence of art quietly contradicts the fleeting, intimate act it sets out to preserve. The cast keeps the shape; it can never keep the feeling.
